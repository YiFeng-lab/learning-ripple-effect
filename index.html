AI & THE LEARNING EXPERIENCE

How does AI
shape learning?

This project explores university students’ perceptions of generative AI: understanding its limitations, using personalized feedback and building digital competence, alongside concerns about accuracy, over-reliance and social interaction.

The original analysis draws on 131 responses in the publicly shared Chatbots’ Impact on University Learning dataset. Concentric circles make agreement levels and use frequency visible together; demographic filters support exploration across groups.
